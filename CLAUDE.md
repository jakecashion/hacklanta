# Hacklanta

Hackathon repo. Prioritize rapid development over polish.

Read `IDEAS.md` before doing anything. It holds the team's ideas and the rules for how agents add or edit them. Follow its "Instructions for Claude agents" section.

## Tracks
- **Social Good** (sponsored by COX): build for the people and places that get overlooked: access, safety, mobility, and the everyday system communities run on.
- **Creative** (sponsored by Adobe): make something people feel: interactive art, music, games, and design tools that put new ideas on screen.
- **AI/ML**: put models to work: agents, assistants, and data pipelines that take real tasks off someone's plate.
- **Fintech**: rethink how money moves: payments, budgeting, investing, and the tools that make finances easier to manage.

## Judging
Criteria:
- Does the project address the track's prompt?
- How polished is the prototype or demo?
- How effective is the team's presentation?
- Is there a clear, compelling use case?

Procedure:
- 3 minutes to pitch, 2 minutes for Q&A.
- Judging room has desks with 2 judges each. Wait in line until Hacklanta staff call you to an open table.
- After presenting, exit the room (follow staff) to keep the flow steady.
- Closing ceremony is hosted shortly after deliberation, at 12:45 PM in the State Ballroom.
